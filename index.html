six steps. none of them yours to sign.

04 · under the floor

      
real tech under the toy.

      

        
01simulated firstevery launch runs on solana mainnet as a simulation with pump.fun's own sdk before it is sent. the cost is measured, not guessed.

        
02funded to the lamportthe launcher gets exactly what the create costs, inside the same transaction, and ends at zero.

        
03gasless claimsyou sign the claim in your browser and the treasury pays the fee. the server checks every signature and relays plain claims only.

        
04self-hosted metadatathe coin's image and metadata are served from this site, not from a third party.

        
05a page that talks to itselfno analytics, no cookies, no outside scripts or fonts. the security policy forbids any other host. scan.exe shows it, live.

07 · honest about it

      
what stays public.

      
everything on solana is public: the coin, its trades, the creator address, the launch wallet, and the fact that the dao treasury paid for it.

      
what is not public is any link from those to you, unless you make one. your IP reaches our host like it does every website; for the daily limit only, a salted hash of it is kept for a day, then deleted.

      
memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. dao is a tool, not financial advice, and it is not affiliated with pump.fun.
